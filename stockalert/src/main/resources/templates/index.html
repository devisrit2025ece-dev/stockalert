Welcome to StockAlert 👋

StockAlert is a retail inventory management system designed to monitor stock levels, track stock movements, identify fast-moving products and generate reorder alerts when inventory reaches the defined threshold.

Use the navigation menu to manage products, record stock movements, monitor reorder alerts and view fast-moving products.
